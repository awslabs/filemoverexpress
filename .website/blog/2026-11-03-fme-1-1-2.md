File Mover Express 1.1.2 adds a new way to authenticate transfer profiles,
smarter and more reliable high-speed transfers, and a refreshed toolchain under
the hood.

<!-- truncate -->

## Access-key authentication for transfer profiles

- **Authenticate with an AWS access key** directly on a transfer profile, as an
  alternative to a named AWS profile or OIDC single sign-on. Session tokens are
  supported for users who can only vend temporary credentials.
- Stored credentials are encrypted at rest, and on Linux the encryption key is
  sealed to the machine's TPM.
- Access-key auth is restricted to local daemons. Remote daemons continue to use
  named profiles or OIDC.

## Smarter, more reliable high-speed transfers

- **Adaptive concurrency (opt-in)** automatically tunes how many parallel
  connections FME uses for the shape of your transfer, so you get strong
  throughput without hand-tuning thread and connection counts. Enable it in your
  configuration.
- **ENOBUFS-aware retry backoff** makes very high connection-count transfers
  recover cleanly when the operating system temporarily runs out of network
  buffers, instead of failing files. This is what lets FME finish reliably on
  high-bandwidth links.

## Quality-of-life

- **Window size, position, and maximized state now persist** across restarts.

## Under the hood

- Upgraded to Wails 3.1.0 (the first stable Wails 3 release) and the Go 1.27.3
  toolchain.
